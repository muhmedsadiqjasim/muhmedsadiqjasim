### 👨‍💻 About Me

I'm a **Computer Networks Engineering** student at **Al-Nahrain University** (Baghdad, Iraq) passionate about bridging **enterprise networking**, **infrastructure automation**, and **self-hosted AI**.

- 🌐 **Networking & Telecom:** Hands-on experience with Cisco IOS routing & switching, **OSPF, MP-BGP, IS-IS, MPLS, IPsec VPNs**, and **Zone-Based Policy Firewalls (ZBFW)**. Completed a Summer Traineeship at **Nokia** focused on service provider architectures and dynamic routing.
- 🤖 **Network Automation & AI:** Built [**NetAssist**](https://github.com/muhmedsadiqjasim/llm-network-troubleshooting-assistant) — a self-hosted LLM assistant using **Ollama, FastAPI, Gradio, and Netmiko** to diagnose multi-protocol enterprise network faults over read-only SSH without leaking sensitive configs to external clouds.
- 🖥️ **Systems & Virtualization:** Experienced in **Windows Server** administration (AD DS, DNS, DHCP, GPO, Hyper-V, WDS, WSUS), **Linux**, and virtualization platforms (**Proxmox VE, VMware, EVE-NG, GNS3**).
- 🎯 **Currently Focusing On:** Advancing toward **Cisco CCNP ENCOR & ENARSI** and building automated, AI-assisted network engineering workflows.
